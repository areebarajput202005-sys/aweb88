## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-fashion, editorial e-commerce atmosphere that transforms digital retail into a gallery-grade lookbook experience. Built around an aesthetic of restrained indulgence, it speaks to an audience that values architectural silhouette, craftsmanship, and modern distinction.

The design philosophy unites:
- **Haute Couture Editorial Rigor:** Dramatic typographic scale contrast, evocative editorial pacing, and expansive negative space.
- **Minimalist Tactility:** Near-zero elevation layered with ultra-fine metallic micro-borders and satin finishes.
- **Onyx Depth:** An absolute dark-mode foundation where pure black surfaces act as exhibition stages for sculptural footwear silhouettes and champagne gold accents.

## Colors

The palette embraces the drama of deep night pierced by luminous metallics and pristine warm neutrals:

- **Primary (`#D4AF37`)**: Champagne Gilt. Used selectively for key luxury anchors, focal interactive accents, delicate indicator markers, and brand flourishes. It is never splashed indiscriminately; its rarity maintains its value.
- **Secondary (`#C5A880`)**: Muted Brushed Brass. Serves as a subdued companion to the primary gold, perfect for secondary interactive states, understated tags, and subtle focus outlines.
- **Tertiary (`#F4EFEB`)**: Warm Stone Ivory. Provides crisp, high-contrast legibility for display titles, key product stats, and high-fashion editorial pull quotes against dark backdrops.
- **Neutral (`#0F0F11`)**: Deep Onyx. The primary canvas substrate. Supported by subtle tiers:
  - Surface Foundation: `#0F0F11` (Deep Onyx)
  - Surface Raised / Card Canvas: `#161619` (Obsidian Satin)
  - Surface Overlay: `#1F1F24` (Smoked Charcoal)
  - Micro-Borders: `#2A2A2E` (Charcoal Filament)

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional juxtaposition between grand architectural serifs and ultra-clean geometric sans serifs:

- **Headlines & Display (`Bodoni Moda`):** Delivers razor-sharp vertical stress and expressive high-contrast hairlines, evocative of high-fashion print monographs. Used in standard sentence case or restrained title case to project cultured quietude.
- **Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its crisp clarity and contemporary geometry. Retains open counters and a light, unburdened presence on dark backgrounds.
- **Labels & Micro-UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Always transformed into strict uppercase with wide tracking (`0.18em` to `0.25em`). This treatment brings immediate structural balance to navigation bars, buttons, curation tags, and tabular specifications.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is driven by generous, intentional breathing room and asymmetry reminiscent of editorial fashion spreads:

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column layout on desktop (`1440px+` max boundary, `gutter-lg: 2.5rem`, `margin-desktop: 4rem`), transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet (`gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 2rem`), and a 4-column layout on mobile (`gutter-sm: 1rem`, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`).
- **Lookbook Rhythm:** Content alternates between tight architectural groupings and vast negative voids. Product hero shots occupy full-bleed or wide multi-column spans, offset by slender text blocks.
- **Component Padding Scale:** 
  - `space-xs` (0.375rem) and `space-sm` (0.75rem) handle compact internal element spacing (input field internals, badge padding).
  - `space-md` (1.25rem) governs standard component buffers and list row separations.
  - `space-lg` (2.25rem) and `space-xl` (4rem) establish structural voids between editorial narrative blocks and collection showcases.

## Elevation & Depth

In alignment with minimalist luxury, heavy drop shadows and skeuomorphic blurs are avoided. Depth is achieved via tonal stratification and razor-thin outlines:

- **Micro-Border Framing:** Surfaces separate via `1px` solid borders in Charcoal Filament (`#2A2A2E`). On interactive states or hover, this border shifts to Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`) at reduced opacity (`0.4`).
- **Tonal Layering:** 
  - Base canvas sits at `#0F0F11`.
  - Cards, drawers, and modal panels rise to `#161619`.
  - Popovers, elevated cart sheets, and dropdowns rest at `#1F1F24`.
- **Gilded Ambient Sheen:** Where floating modal layers or sticky purchase bars need definitive separation from underlying media, an ultra-subtle, deep atmospheric shadow is deployed: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a crisp, tailored architectural profile through a roundedness level of `1` (Soft):

- **Core Elements:** Buttons, cards, form inputs, and image enclosures receive an exacting `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius. This prevents the severe starkness of pure 0px edges while remaining sharp, structured, and deliberate.
- **Extended Elements:** Modals and flyout surfaces use `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`), maintaining structural discipline without softening into consumer playfulness.
- **Pills and Circles:** Reserved exclusively for circular color swatch selectors and small numeric counter pips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid background in Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`) with Deep Onyx text (`#0F0F11`), styled with `label-md` uppercase typography. Height is 52px with horizontal padding of `space-lg`. On hover, the background brightens subtly to `#E5C460` with a smooth 200ms ease.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with a `1px` solid Charcoal Filament border (`#2A2A2E`) and Warm Stone Ivory text (`#F4EFEB`). On hover, the border transitions to Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`) with text color following suit.
- **Ghost Action:** Text-only in Ivory with generous tracking and an underlined hover rule that expands outward from center using a `1px` stroke of `#D4AF37`.

### Cards & Lookbook Tiles
- **Structure:** Surface background set to `#161619` bounded by a `1px` border of `#2A2A2E`. Corner radius `0.25rem`.
- **Image Container:** Aspect ratios of 4:5 (portrait lookbook) or 1:1 (catalog). Image sits flush to the top and sides, treated with a subtle desaturation that warms into natural color tones on hover.
- **Hover State:** The card boundary shifts to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)` accompanied by a subtle image zoom (`scale(1.02)`) over a 400ms cubic-bezier transition.

### Input Fields
- **Container:** Dark field (`#161619`) with `1px` border in `#2A2A2E` and `0.25rem` radius. Height is 52px with `space-md` internal padding.
- **Typography:** Placeholder text rendered in muted slate (`#6E6E77`) using `body-md`. Entered value appears in Warm Stone Ivory (`#F4EFEB`).
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to Brushed Brass (`#C5A880`) accompanied by an ambient inner glow `0 0 0 1px #C5A880`.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Aesthetic:** Minimalist monolithic geometry. Compact height (24px), tracked uppercase `label-sm` font.
- **Limited Edition / Atelier:** Bordered badge in `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.5)` with gold typography on `#1A1813` translucent background.
- **Standard Tag:** `#1F1F24` background with Ivory typography and `1px` `#2A2A2E` boundary.

### Selection Controls (Size, Radio, Checkbox)
- **Size Selector Tiles:** Architectural square chips (44px x 44px) in `#161619` with `1px` Charcoal Filament border. Active state fills with Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`) and deep onyx typography.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimal `16px` enclosures with `1px` borders. Checked state features a refined gold core mark with zero drop-shadowing.

### Additional Luxury Patterns
- **Sticky Atelier Bar:** A bottom-docked or header-docked purchase dock utilizing a frosted smoked surface (`rgba(15, 15, 17, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) bound by an upper hairline border in `#2A2A2E`.
- **Material & Craft Specs Drawer:** Split-column technical specification rows separated by hairline dividers, displaying shoe lasts, upper leather origins, and welt construction in uppercase `label-md`.
